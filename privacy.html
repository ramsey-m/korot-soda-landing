Korot Soda

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 6, 2026

Korot Soda ("the app") is a multiplayer party card game. We built it to collect as little about you as possible. This page explains what the app stores and why.

What we collect

  • An anonymous account. When you open the app we create an anonymous user ID (a random identifier) so you can join game rooms. No email, phone number, password, or real name is required or collected.
  • Your nickname. The display name you type is shown to the other players in your game room and stored with your anonymous account. It can be anything — it does not need to identify you.
  • Gameplay data. Rooms you create or join, the cards you play in a round, scores, and running totals (games played, rounds won, games won). This is what makes the game work.

What we do NOT collect

  • No real names, emails, or phone numbers.
  • No contacts, location, camera, or microphone access.
  • No advertising identifiers and no ad networks.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Where data is stored

Game data is stored with our backend provider, Supabase, which hosts the database and anonymous authentication. Data is transmitted over HTTPS.

Data sharing

We do not sell or share your data with anyone. Your nickname and plays are visible only to the players in the same game room.

Data retention & deletion

Anonymous accounts and their gameplay data persist so your stats survive app restarts. If you want your data deleted, contact us (below) with your in-app nickname and approximate play dates, or simply uninstall the app — an abandoned anonymous account contains nothing that identifies you.

Children

Korot Soda contains crude humor and is intended for adults (18+). It is not directed at children.
